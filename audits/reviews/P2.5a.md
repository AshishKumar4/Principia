## Adversarial findings

1. **Time reflection, sign, and involutivity: PASS.** `timeReflectionCLM` subtracts twice
   the time component, and the equivalence uses the same map as its inverse
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:127-162`). The bridge is exact:
   `⟪v, T w⟫ = η(v,w)` (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:180-184`). Therefore
   `𝓕η f p = 𝓕 f (T p)` has kernel `𝐞(-η(a,p))`
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:238-249`,
   `Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:269-272`). A fresh explicit-vector probe
   gives `+6` for `a = 2∂₀`, `p = 3∂₀`. Replacing `T` by `-T` gives `-6`. The committed
   probe also proves that `-T` produces `-η`, then rejects it
   (`audits/probes/P2.5a/time_flip_sign_probe.lean:48-67`). The spacetime and Schwartz
   involutions are both kernel proofs (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:150-162`,
   `Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:196-214`).

2. **The cone is the correct topological closure: PASS.** The frozen open cone uses
   strict inequalities. The frozen causal cone uses the non-strict quadratic inequality
   with `0 < v⁰` (`Atlas/Specs/Spacetime/Minkowski.lean:137-145`). The draft inserts the
   origin and proves the exact characterization
   `η(v,v) ≤ 0 ∧ 0 ≤ v⁰` (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:285-328`). Its
   closedness proof rewrites that same set as two non-strict continuous inequalities
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:332-339`). A fresh probe proves the stronger
   identity
   `closedForwardCone = closure {v | InFutureTimeCone v}`. Thus `insert 0` is extensionally
   the topological closure, not a nearby closed set.

3. **The massless boundary is present: PASS.** The committed witnesses place both the
   apex and `∂₀ + ∂₁` in the cone, while the open time cone rejects the null vector
   (`Atlas/Witnesses/WightmanUtilities.lean:198-221`). The normalization probe places the
   full mass shell and its Fourier rescaling in the same cone
   (`audits/probes/P2.5a/fourier_normalization_probe.lean:67-96`). A fresh `m = 0` probe
   proves `η(p(k),p(k)) = 0` and `p(0) = 0`. Nonzero massless modes lie on the null
   boundary, and the zero mode is the included apex.

4. **`𝓝ˢ` has the intended pointwise neighborhood meaning: PASS.** The distributional
   predicate asks for one set in the neighborhood filter of the full cone
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:385-386`). This does not require one uniform
   radius. A fresh probe proves
   `s ∈ 𝓝ˢ closedForwardCone ↔ ∀ p ∈ closedForwardCone, s ∈ 𝓝 p`. A second fresh probe
   proves that the `Set.EqOn` version is equivalent to pointwise local vanishing. The
   global distributional definition also implies pointwise local vanishing by reusing
   the same set. The reverse distributional theorem needs locality and a partition of
   unity. The draft records that proof boundary and does not claim the converse
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:388-395`). Therefore there is no
   neighborhood-filter mismatch. A future caller with only pointwise distributional
   premises must first prove the locality glue.

5. **The bounded integrability hypothesis matches unitary orbits: PASS.** The utility
   accepts a continuous orbit and a global norm bound
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:443-449`). The frozen representation exposes
   continuous orbit maps (`Atlas/Specs/Spacetime/PoincareRep.lean:183-185`) and points to
   `Unitary.norm_map` for norm preservation
   (`Atlas/Specs/Spacetime/PoincareRep.lean:129-132`). A fresh downstream-shaped probe
   instantiates the utility with `Ψ(a) = U(⟨a,1⟩)ψ` and `C = ‖ψ‖`. It compiles without an
   adapter or a stronger continuity premise.

6. **The witnesses reject the neighboring wrong definitions: PASS.** The time-asymmetric
   bump has values `1` at `∂₀` and `0` at `-∂₀`
   (`Atlas/Witnesses/WightmanUtilities.lean:136-145`). It separates `𝓕η` from Mathlib's
   Euclidean transform (`Atlas/Witnesses/WightmanUtilities.lean:159-189`). The explicit
   pairing probe rejects a sign-flipped `-T`
   (`audits/probes/P2.5a/time_flip_sign_probe.lean:57-67`). The apex and null witnesses
   reject punctured and open variants (`audits/probes/P2.5a/cone_closure_probe.lean:27-44`).
   The delta probe gives the sharper discriminator: `δ₀` passes the open-cone hypothesis
   and fails the closed-cone hypothesis
   (`audits/probes/P2.5a/vanishing_cone_probe.lean:54-104`). The disclosed bump-for-Gaussian
   substitution preserves these discriminators (`Atlas/Witnesses/WightmanUtilities.lean:23-31`).

7. **Namespace and frozen-surface reuse are consistent: PASS.** The draft extends
   `Spacetime.Minkowski`, which is the frozen namespace
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:113-115`,
   `Atlas/Specs/Spacetime/Minkowski.lean:72-75`). It reuses the frozen
   `SpacelikeSeparated` declaration instead of adding an alias
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:47-58`,
   `Atlas/Specs/Spacetime/Minkowski.lean:296-297`). The cited causal anchor exists under
   the stated name (`Atlas/Witnesses/MinkowskiCausal.lean:449-452`). I found no naming or
   namespace drift.

8. **The draft covers the dossier's P2.5a scope: PASS.** The dossier requires the
   time-flipped Schwartz Fourier equivalence, the closed cone, the distributional
   support form, and one integrability lemma
   (`docs/dossiers/P2-wightman-design.md:17-20`,
   `docs/dossiers/P2-wightman-design.md:55-56`). It also requires reuse of Mathlib's
   `IsVanishingOn` and `dsupport` APIs (`docs/dossiers/P2-wightman-design.md:86-88`). The
   draft supplies those utilities at
   `Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:238-272`,
   `Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:285-369`, and
   `Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:385-449`. It leaves the Wightman structure and
   the spectrum axiom for P2.5b, as the package states
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:60-61`). I found no silent scope drop.
