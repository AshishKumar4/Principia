## Repair verification

1. **Inverse affine Schwartz action and group order: PASS.** The point action is
   `g⁻¹ • x = Λ⁻¹ (x - a)` (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:502-505`).
   The Schwartz operator uses that argument and proves
   `(g ▷ f)(x) = f (g⁻¹ • x)`
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:514-525`). Its group law is
   `▷(g₁ * g₂) = ▷g₁ ∘ ▷g₂`
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:547-552`). This order follows from
   `(g₁ * g₂)⁻¹ = g₂⁻¹ * g₁⁻¹`. The inverse CLE and monoid hom use the same law
   (`Atlas/Specs/QFT/WightmanUtilities.lean:580-603`). The new mixed probe uses a
   translation and a spatial rotation. It gets `1` for the product action and `0`
   for the reversed composite
   (`audits/probes/P2.5a/poincare_schwartz_action_probe.lean:159-190`). It also
   rejects the forward affine pullback as an operator and as a homomorphism
   (`audits/probes/P2.5a/poincare_schwartz_action_probe.lean:102-156`). Pure
   translations do not carry the group-order proof. The witness still checks the
   identity, a nontrivial translation, inverse round-trip, multiplication, and the
   monoid-hom package (`Atlas/Witnesses/WightmanUtilities.lean:371-423`).

2. **Character periodicity and the Stone sign: PASS.** The repaired probe first proves
   `𝐞(1) = 𝐞(-1)`. Thus, the old exponent-argument test is explicitly rejected
   (`audits/probes/P2.5a/time_flip_sign_probe.lean:107-124`). It then proves
   `𝐞(1/4) = I`, `𝐞(-1/4) = -I`, and character-level inequality
   (`audits/probes/P2.5a/time_flip_sign_probe.lean:128-143`). For
   `a = t∂₀` and `p = p⁰∂₀`, the selected kernel has `-η(a,p) = t p⁰`.
   With `E = 2πp⁰`, this gives `𝐞(t p⁰) = exp(+iEt)`
   (`audits/probes/P2.5a/time_flip_sign_probe.lean:147-190`). The committed point
   `t = p⁰ = 1/2` lies in the forward cone and gives `I`, while the reversed sign
   gives `-I` (`audits/probes/P2.5a/time_flip_sign_probe.lean:156-219`). This matches
   the frozen convention `U(t) = exp(+itH)`
   (`Atlas/Specs/OperatorTheory/UnitaryGroup.lean:26-29`).
