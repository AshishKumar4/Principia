the Fock second-quantization and `L^p` pullback developments" and "lives here so

`ShellPoincareRepresentation` (a `#print axioms` probe through that import fails
with unknown constant), and `ShellOneParticle.lean:58` and
`ShellPoincareRepresentation.lean:78-79` both state the lemma is not used.
The extraction itself is fine; the present-tense consumer claim is not. Required
correction: name the actual consumer (Fock second-quantization) and drop or
re-tense the `L^p` half. Non-blocking.
